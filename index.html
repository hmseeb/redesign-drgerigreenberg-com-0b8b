Why patients come to Dr. Geri

When pain takes away the things you love, you want a natural way back.

Sometimes pain is so bad that you can't do the things you used to do — housework, driving, playing sports. Or you simply can't sleep from pain and anxiety. Either way, it's limiting your pleasure in life.

Maybe the traditional route of drugs, surgery, or physical therapy isn't for you. What you really want is a natural solution that makes it possible for you to play with your grandkids again, enjoy your work, have a good night's sleep, eat well, hike, dance or get back to whatever brings you joy in life.

To help you return to normal activities as quickly as possible, Dr. Geri Greenberg provides five healing methods in one office. With this combination she treats her patients holistically, so they experience quicker healing in a calm environment.

The five healing methods

Five disciplines, one treatment plan

Most patients need more than one approach. Because Dr. Geri practices all five herself, your care is coordinated by a single doctor who sees the whole picture — not passed between offices.

METHOD 01

Chiropractic

Gentle spinal and joint care that restores alignment, mobility and normal circulation — relieving back pain, sciatica, neck pain and stiffness without medication or surgery.

METHOD 02

Acupuncture

Fine-needle treatment that releases blocked qi and circulation, balancing the meridian system to calm pain, stress, insomnia, allergies and hormonal fluctuations.

METHOD 03

Deep Muscle Therapy

Hands-on soft-tissue work that releases muscle tension, spasm and congested circulation — especially effective for tendonitis, sports injuries and cramping.

METHOD 04

Chinese Herbology

Specific Chinese herbal formulas chosen for your constitution and the season, used to harmonize liver qi, support immunity, aid digestion and balance emotional health.

METHOD 05

Traditional Chinese Medicine

Diagnostic tools such as the meridian clock and seasonal theory guide your care, so treatment addresses the underlying imbalance — not only the symptom.

Reducing stress through the five senses

Simple practices Dr. Geri recommends between visits.

Seeing

Look into the distance instead of at a screen — it balances the eye muscles and clears both inner and outer vision.

Hearing

Soothing music, the sounds of nature, or your own deep breaths in silence naturally calm the nervous system.

Taste

Berries, bananas and apples are naturally calming, as are chamomile and mint tea.

Touch

The entire body is represented on the ears, so massaging your ears is an easy way to relax.

Smell

Lavender calms the mind and anxiety; grapefruit and mint clear frustration and help you re-focus.

Slow hobbies

Walking, swimming, yoga, tai chi, meditation and short naps — plus gardening, painting, music, photography and reading.
